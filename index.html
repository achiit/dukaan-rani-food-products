⚙️ Manufacturing Process

How We Make Premium Maize Products

A transparent look at our 6-step manufacturing process that ensures every product meets the highest food-grade quality standards.

Step 1: Raw Maize Procurement & Selection

We source premium quality maize from trusted farmers and procurement agents in Gujarat and neighbouring states. Each consignment undergoes visual inspection for colour, size uniformity, aflatoxin levels and moisture content before being accepted.

🌽
🧹

Step 2: Pre-Cleaning & Sorting

Raw maize is fed into our pre-cleaner machines which use vibrating sieves and air aspiration to remove stones, dust, chaff and oversized kernels. This initial cleaning step is critical for all downstream processing quality.

Step 3: Main Processing

Cleaned maize enters the main processing unit where it is dehulled, de-germed and processed into the desired product — Makai Poha flaking, Tukdi cracking or grain sizing for Broken Maize.

⚙️
☀️

Step 4: Drying & Conditioning

Processed product is passed through our drying system to achieve the ideal moisture level for each product grade. Proper drying is essential for shelf life and product integrity during transit.

Step 5: Quality Testing & Grading

Before packaging, every batch is tested for moisture content, colour uniformity, broken percentage, foreign matter and other specifications. Only batches meeting our thresholds proceed to packaging.

🔬
📦

Step 6: Hygienic Packaging & Dispatch

Approved products are packed in food-grade PP woven bags (25kg / 50kg) with lot numbers printed. Packed bags are stored in our clean warehouse and dispatched on customer schedule via trusted logistics partners.
